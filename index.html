Frequently Asked Questions

What's your typical website development timeline?

Most projects are completed within 4-6 weeks, depending on complexity. We follow a structured process: discovery (1 week), design (1-2 weeks), development (2-3 weeks), and testing/launch (1 week).

What's included in your maintenance packages?

Our comprehensive maintenance includes regular updates, security monitoring, daily backups, performance optimization, and priority support. We offer flexible packages starting from £99/month.

Do you provide SEO optimization?

Yes, all our websites come with built-in SEO best practices, including optimized code, fast loading times, and mobile-first design. We also offer advanced SEO packages for additional optimization.

Can I update the website content myself?

Absolutely! We build all websites with user-friendly content management systems that make updates simple. We also provide training and documentation for your team.
